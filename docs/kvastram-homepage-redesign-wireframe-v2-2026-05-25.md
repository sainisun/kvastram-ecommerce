# Kvastram Homepage Redesign Wireframe V2

Date: 2026-05-25  
Page: `https://kvastram.com/` homepage  
Direction: Kvastram Jaipur Edit  
Purpose: Final standalone redesign wireframe for desktop and mobile implementation.

## Core Requirements

- No existing homepage section should be removed.
- Mobile circular categories must appear above the hero like Instagram stories.
- Mobile circular categories must have no title, heading, or intro copy.
- A separate Shopify-inspired `Shop by category` section must appear below compact trust chips.
- Homepage should feel premium, handmade, conversion-focused, and consistent.
- Animations should be subtle, fast, and performance-safe.

## Interaction And Animation Rules

- Section reveal: soft fade + 12px upward movement, 280-420ms.
- Product card hover: small lift, subtle shadow, optional second image reveal.
- Button hover: color shift + 1px lift.
- Add-to-cart: button changes to `Added` briefly; cart count gets small pulse.
- Wishlist: heart fills with a calm transition.
- Reels: snap scrolling; no mobile auto-scroll.
- Header: compact sticky transition after scroll.
- FAQ: accordion height transition and icon rotation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not use heavy parallax, typewriter text, floating decoration, or auto-moving category rows.

## Mobile Story Category Row Rules

- Mobile only.
- Directly below mobile header.
- Above hero.
- No title, no heading, no intro copy.
- Horizontal scroll with snap.
- Circular image thumbnails.
- Tap opens category/collection.
- Recommended labels: Jackets, Kimonos, Bags, Quilts, Pouches, Gifts, Sale.

## Shop By Category Rules

- Separate from mobile story row.
- Appears below trust chips on mobile.
- Appears below trust bar on desktop.
- Shopify-inspired structured grid.
- Desktop: 4-column or 2-row grid.
- Mobile: 2-column image-first grid.
- Recommended cards: Jackets, Kimonos, Tote Bags, Toiletry Bags, Kantha Quilts, Block Print, Gifts, Sale.

## Implementation Checklist

- Add mobile story category row above hero.
- Add structured `Shop by category` section below trust chips.
- Shorten homepage product display names.
- Normalize product card image crops.
- Hide unready products/reels from homepage.
- Redesign reels as premium 9:16 cards.
- Add WhatsApp help strip after first product grid.
- Rewrite craft promise as buyer-facing proof.
- Add craft education, fit help, and mini FAQ sections.
- QA at 360, 390, 414, 768, and 1440 widths.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No existing homepage section is removed.
- Mobile story category row is above hero and has no heading/title.
- Separate `Shop by category` section exists below trust chips.
- First mobile viewport quickly communicates category, product type, and trust.
- Product grids feel curated and consistent.
- No placeholder product or reel names are visible.
- Motion is subtle, accessible, and does not delay shopping.
