## First, The Most Important Insight

Your buyers are NOT Indian. They are **Western consumers** (US, UK, EU, AU) who:
- Value **story and craft** over price
- Want to feel **ethically good** about their purchase
- Need **extra trust** because they're buying from a country they've never visited
- Shop by **aesthetic and occasion** (bohemian, boho-chic, festival, gifting)
- Will pay **$80–$250+** for a piece if they believe the story
- Specifically search: "kantha quilt", "block print dress", "handmade Indian bag", "fair trade clothing India"

Everything about your store must be redesigned for this buyer — not for an Indian buyer who already knows what kantha is.

## 6. PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY — Western Buyers Need Different Images

### What Converts Western Buyers:
- **Lifestyle photography in Western settings** — quilt on a US-style bed, bag being carried at a farmers market, dress worn outdoors
- **Close-up craft detail shots** — showing the actual kantha stitching, block print texture, hand embroidery
- **Flat lay shots** — showing full product dimensions clearly
- **Person holding next to body** — for scale/size reference

- Studio shots on mannequins or plain backgrounds (looks like Alibaba)
- Indian models/settings only (Western buyers can't envision in their home)
- No detail shots of the craft/texture

### Anchal Project does this perfectly:
- Multiple angles per product (full view, folded detail, stitching close-up, lifestyle)
- Artisan hands shown making the product
- Items shown in real home settings (not studio backdrops)

### Fix:
- Reshoot hero products with lifestyle shots in neutral/Western home settings
- Add at minimum: 1 close-up of craft detail per product
- Add 1 "artisan making it" photo per collection (can be shared across products)
- Show size reference photo (product next to common object or held by person)

## 7. PRODUCT PRESENTATION FOR WESTERN BUYERS

### Missing Information That Western Buyers Need:

**7.1 Care Instructions** (CRITICAL for international buyers)
"Hand wash cold, lay flat to dry, colours may bleed first wash" — Western buyers don't know how to care for kantha/block print. This is a major reason they hesitate to buy.
- **Fix:** Add care instructions to every product page, prominently, with icons

**7.2 Exact Measurements in Inches** (not just cm)
Western buyers measure in inches. "Length: 42 inches / 107cm", "Fits US size 8–10"
- **Fix:** All sizing in inches first, cm second

**7.3 "One of a Kind" Indicator**
For handmade items, indicate "each piece is unique — slight variations in colour and pattern are part of the handmade process"
- **Fix:** Add this note to all handmade product descriptions

**7.4 Material Sourcing Story**
"Made from recycled vintage saris" (Anchal), "handspun khadi cotton" (Cotton Rack) — tells where material comes from
- **Fix:** Add material origin to every product ("Cotton sourced from Rajasthan, hand-dyed with natural indigo")

**7.5 "Is This Really Handmade?" Social Proof**
Western buyers are sceptical of "handmade" claims from Indian sellers after decades of mass-market counterfeits
- **Fix:** Add artisan photo/name to product page, show process photo, add "authenticity guarantee" text with explanation
